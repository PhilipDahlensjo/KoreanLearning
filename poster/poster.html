Description (One sentence)

A small web app for learning Korean through multiple-choice quizzes on Hangul, everyday phrases and grammar, where you can also add, edit and delete your own phrases.

Key Features

  • Three quizzes: Hangul (listen and pick the letter), Everyday Phrases and Grammar. Shuffled questions, instant right/wrong feedback and a result page.
  • Manage phrases CRUD Add, edit and delete your own phrases. They are saved in the browser and used in the quiz.
  • Auto-translate: write a phrase in English and get the Korean and the romanization filled in.
  • Natural Korean audio (Google Text-to-Speech) with replay and 🐢 slow mode.
  • Learning help: hints, explanations after each answer and a daily grammar tip.
